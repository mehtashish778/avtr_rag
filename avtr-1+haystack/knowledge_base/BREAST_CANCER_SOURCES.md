# Breast-cancer corpus provenance

This corpus contains concise, original paraphrases of public health information
from WHO, CDC, USPSTF, and the US National Cancer Institute. Each document has a
stable `DOC_ID`, source URLs, and the review date `2026-08-25`.

The corpus is for RAG evaluation and general education only. It is not a clinical
decision-support system. It deliberately excludes patient-specific diagnosis,
prognosis, medicine selection, and dosing. Screening recommendations are labeled
with their jurisdiction because national guidance can differ.

Primary sources:

- https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/breast-cancer
- https://www.cdc.gov/breast-cancer/symptoms/index.html
- https://www.cdc.gov/breast-cancer/risk-factors/index.html
- https://www.cdc.gov/breast-cancer/screening/index.html
- https://www.uspreventiveservicestaskforce.org/uspstf/recommendation/breast-cancer-screening
- https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/diagnosis
- https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/diagnosis/breast-cancer-biomarker-tests
- https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/treatment/surgery
- https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/treatment/radiation
- https://www.cancer.gov/types/breast/breast-cancer-survivorship
